# Operator guide — PNG

Portal: no live, bespoke, self-service transactional e-procurement
portal was confirmed operational this session -- the National
Procurement Commission (NPC, `npc.gov.pg`) is the information hub for
tender advertisements and Electronic Government Procurement (e-GP)
initiatives, and its own site currently shows an active RFP
(NPC/2026-26) to build an e-GP system. Entity: Investment Promotion
Authority (IPA) business/company registration (Companies Act 1997)
plus Internal Revenue Commission (IRC) Tax Identification Number (TIN)
record (TIN1/TIN2). Foreign-owned engagements (Investment Promotion Act
1992, s.28: wholly owned or >= 50 percent owned/controlled/managed by
non-citizens) must also clear the Act's own Foreign Enterprise
Certificate requirement -- a filing that declares itself exempt while
its own declared foreign-ownership meets/exceeds the 50 percent
threshold is held. Human-gated filing only.
