# Business model — PNG

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for the Independent
State of Papua New Guinea.

- National Procurement Commission (NPC), a Statutory Body under the
  National Procurement Act 2018 -- public procurement, per the
  Commission's own certified National Procurement (Amendment) Act 2021
  PDF; own Section 26(1) (as substituted) sets a multi-tier
  procurement-value-threshold ladder (K50,000 and under: simplified,
  self-procured; K50,001-K1,000,000: standardised, self-procured;
  above K1,000,000: procured on the body's behalf by the Commission;
  Provincial/District/Special Committees of the Board: K10,000,000 /
  K5,000,000 / K5,000,000 respectively); no live, bespoke, self-service
  transactional e-procurement portal was confirmed operational this
  session -- NPC's own site shows an active RFP (NPC/2026-26) to BUILD
  one
- Investment Promotion Authority (IPA), through its Companies Office --
  business/company registration under the Companies Act 1997, Business
  Names Act, Business Groups Incorporation Act and Associations
  Incorporation Act, **and**, under a distinct legal basis (Investment
  Promotion Act 1992, s.28), certification of foreign enterprises (a
  business entity wholly owned or 50 percent or more
  owned/controlled/managed by non-citizens) before they may conduct
  business in PNG, with a 14-day filing deadline after Companies Act
  1997 incorporation
- Internal Revenue Commission (IRC) Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  registration (TIN1 for non-individual entities, TIN2 for
  individuals) -- confirmed via IRC's own archived Taxpayer Guide, the
  live current site being a JavaScript SPA this iteration could not
  query for substantive content
- Investment Promotion Act 1992 s.28 Foreign Enterprise Certificate
  ownership-threshold gate -- bars a filing that declares itself
  exempt from certification when its own declared foreign-ownership
  percentage actually meets or exceeds the Act's own 50 percent
  threshold
